# Curly quotes, dashes and accents

Word and most web pages use "smart" punctuation. Diamond shows each as its plain look-alike, and saves it unchanged.

“Double curly quotes,” she said, ‘and single ones.’ It’s a test.

An en dash: 1990–2026. An em dash — like this — for a pause. An ellipsis…

Accented letters: café, naïve, résumé, señor, München, François, Ångström, Søren.

A bullet • and a middle dot · and a no-break space.

Things with no look-alike show as ?: € (euro), → (arrow), π (pi), ☃ (snowman).
